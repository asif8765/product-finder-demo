# Product Finder Demo

Standalone demo of a product research tool for resellers in Pakistan. It follows the familiar product-database model (sales estimates from sold counts and reviews, demand vs competition scoring, niche view, tracker) with local layers: social buzz, long-running ads, seasons and COD margin after a Rs 200 courier.

Data: an estimated, anonymised snapshot of the Pakistani e-commerce market (marketplaces plus individual stores), 18 Nov 2026. All sales and earnings figures are estimates. Static site, no tracking; the watchlist is saved only in your browser.
